MeetCat — How to Delete Your Account

MeetCat is a community app for sharing cat sightings, developed by Fuzhou Liangdu Feiteng Network Technology Co., Ltd. (Fuzhou Liangdu Feiteng Network Technology Co., Ltd.).

How to delete your account

  1. Open the MeetCat app and log in.
  2. Tap Settings (Me → Settings).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your deletion request takes effect immediately. There is no need to contact us by email — the in-app flow is the complete and only required step.

What data is deleted

  • Your account information (email, profile name, avatar)
  • Your posts, photos and videos
  • Your chat messages

Retention period

Deleted content is removed from our active systems or anonymized within 30 days after you confirm the deletion.

What may be retained

  • Data required to comply with legal or regulatory obligations
  • Limited transaction or security records, retained only for as long as legally permitted or required

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete your own posts at any time without deleting your account: open the post → tap Delete. You can also clear your profile avatar or name in Settings.

If you have questions about account deletion or data handling, contact: privacy@yumaol.com
